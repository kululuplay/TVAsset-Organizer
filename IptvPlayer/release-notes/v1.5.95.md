Kululu IPTV 1.5.95 adds playback diagnostics and improves movie refresh feedback while retaining the playback, catalog migration and remote-keyboard fixes from 1.5.94.

- Report a playback problem directly from the player's menu. Support receives a report linked to the affected session and available measurements.
- The support centre can compare recent measurements for the same channel across devices, inspect bounded observations around a stall, and show measured startup stages where the player exposes them.
- Support staff can record a correction and compare measurements before and after it. Missing data, pauses and incomplete observations are identified; they are not treated as proof of a healthy stream or a definite cause.
- Movie refresh continues when individual categories fail and identifies incomplete results instead of reporting a false success.
- Existing accounts, favorites, settings and viewing progress are preserved. Live TV/radio remain TS/MPEG-TS only; movies and episodes keep their original file formats.

Android 5.0 or newer. Universal APK with ARM 32/64-bit and x86 32/64-bit support. Production package: `com.iptv.player`; version code: `139`.

Technical playback observations are bounded and sent while the app is in the foreground. They do not include screen/audio recordings or raw media URLs. Diagnostic visibility depends on the device being online and providing measurements; this does not guarantee detection of every freeze or codec/device compatibility.
