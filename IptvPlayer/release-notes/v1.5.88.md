Kululu IPTV 1.5.88 fixes a live-TV audio recovery problem that could cause heavy stuttering on Android sticks.

- Isolated damaged MPEG Layer II audio frames can now recover without replacing hardware video playback with full-software video decoding.
- Recovery preserves audio timing and is strictly limited. Persistent corruption still uses the normal playback error handling.
- Older software-playback recommendations are retried once so affected channels can benefit from the corrected hardware path. Login, favorites, settings and viewing progress are preserved.
- Rapid up/down navigation stays inside the live channel list while rows load and at the bottom, preserving the channel/favorite column. Search remains reachable with a fresh Up press from the first channel.
- Live TV and radio continue to use TS/MPEG-TS only. HLS remains disabled; movies and episodes retain their native file formats.

Validation: the fix passed 621 Android unit tests and lint with no errors. On the Android 11 test stick, the preview candidate recovered three damaged audio frames during real broadcasts without switching video engines. The user confirmed smooth picture and sound. CH+/CH−, arrow-key channel switching, preview/fullscreen transitions and the EPG information dialog were also verified.

One 1.37-second rebuffer was recorded in the first 5-minute session; subsequent completed channel-switch sessions had none. This release fixes the observed decoder recovery problem, but does not claim to eliminate every network/provider interruption or resolve the separate reported VOD audio/video sync issue.

Android 5.0 or newer. Universal APK with ARM 32/64-bit and x86 32/64-bit support. Package: `com.iptv.player`; version code: `132`. Signed with the existing production certificate for upgrades over earlier production releases.

The signed APK is available for manual installation. The existing automatic rollout policy remains paused at 0%, with stable rollout version 1.5.86.
