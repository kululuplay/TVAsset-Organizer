Behebt das Problem, dass nach längerer Nutzung kein Sender mehr startet („Wird geladen…“ ohne Ende), bis der Stick vom Strom getrennt wurde. Die App wartet jetzt höchstens eine Sekunde auf alte Verbindungen und öffnet den Sender dann in jedem Fall; hängende Verbindungen werden nach 10 Sekunden getrennt, und das WLAN bleibt während der Wiedergabe aktiv.

Details for maintainers (not published in the update prompt):

- Provider gate (`ProviderConnectionSafety`, `ProviderStartGatePolicy`, new `ProviderStartGate`): the gate can delay a start but never refuses one. Until 1.6.1 three states were permanent until a Force Stop or power cycle: an unresolved Cast detach (`remoteUncertain`), a 30 s queue wait that timed out (unowned `LOCAL_NATIVE_STOP`), and a controlled process recycle that could not launch or did not replace the process. Now: one wait of at most `DRAIN_WAIT_MS` (1 s) behind the shared native queue (`VlcOps.awaitProviderDrain(timeoutMs, onDrained(drained))`), one controlled process recycle for a proven native hang, and `ProviderConnectionSafety.forceReset()` for everything else (`PROCEED_AFTER_RESET`). The server replaces a stale lease from the same device on the next request (`connection_grace`), which is why a forced start is safe for a one-connection line.
- `PlaybackProcessRecovery.requestIfRequired(..., onNotReplaced)`: every caller (live, preview, VOD, app foreground hook, controller) is told when the recycle did not replace the process within its 6 s watchdog, forces the gate open and continues; a caller that arrives during an in-flight launch shares that watchdog.
- `PlayerController`: `play()` resets the gate itself after a terminal failure or when a stale recovery flag is set (self-healing start); `resume()` rebuilds the stage when a start stepped aside for a recycle that never happened; `quiesce()` (background, Cast preflight) now releases the engine fully instead of stopping it, so nothing (decoder, surface, buffers, socket) stays behind while the screen is stopped. `failClosedForProviderOwnership()` is gone.
- Zombie sockets: the live HTTP source times out after 10 s without bytes (connect and read; were 30 s), bounding how long a retiring engine can hold its lease; both Media3 players use `WAKE_MODE_NETWORK` (Wi-Fi lock while playing).
- Each forced reset writes `ProviderGate: forced open (<where>: <why> -> cleared ...)` to the playback log and a `provider_gate_forced` stability event (severity warn) for the panel.
- Tests: `ProviderStartGatePolicyTest` (new decision table, `decideAfterWait`, no screen-level block from any snapshot), `ProviderConnectionSafetyTest` (`forceReset` contents, late callbacks for cleared tokens ignored, new owners tracked again).
