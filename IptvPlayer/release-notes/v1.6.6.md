Details for maintainers (not published in the update prompt):

Operator's app programme of 25 Nov 2026, measured against the 1.6.5 field data in the support desk.

- Crash reports (`util/CrashReporter.kt`): the crash receiver is not hosted, so no crash ever left the field. Reports now go to the support desk as diagnostic reports beginning "Otomatik rapor" (the channel `SupportClient` already uses), at most four per device per day. On Android 11+ a native crash or an ANR from the system's exit list is reported the same way.
- Remote settings (`util/RemoteConfig.kt`, `remote-config.json`, `scripts/sign_remote_config.py`, `docs/playback-policy.md`): the signed playback policy and the announcement are read from `remote-config.json` on the main branch at foreground start and every 30 minutes. New policy signing key pair (`policy_public_key.pem`); no released app ever received a signed policy, so nothing in the field depended on the old one.
- Film start (`VodPlaybackCoordinator.restartDelayMs`): after a source failure the other engine waits 3 s instead of following the first retry at once. The number of opens is unchanged.
- Error cards (`PlayerActivity.onFatalError`, `LiveErrorMessages`, both player layouts): the live card names the reason with the support code, as the film card does; both cards have a "report playback problem" button. On the live card it sits beside Retry because Up/Down zap.
- Playback speed (`VodPlaybackSpeedPolicy`, `VodPlayerActivity.showSpeedDialog`, `VodEngine.setPlaybackSpeed`): 0.5x to 2x for films and episodes, per item, not saved, not offered while audio passthrough is on. At normal speed no rate command is sent at all.
- Voice search (`SearchActivity`, `VoiceSearchPolicy`, `SearchInputKeyPolicy`): microphone button, shown only where a speech recogniser exists (many Fire TV devices have none).
- Programme reminders (`util/ProgramReminders.kt`, `GuideActivity`): OK on a programme that has not started sets or removes a reminder; a text notice one minute before the start while the app is in front. A closed app shows nothing.
- Watch Next (`watchnext/`): films and episodes in progress appear in the Watch Next row of Android TV / Google TV (at most five, never adult titles, nothing where the row does not exist). A card restarts the app through the splash and opens the title through the in-app Continue Watching screen, so login and PIN checks apply.
- Quality numbers (`VodPlayerActivity.setBuffering`): loading that a seek asked for is no longer counted as a rebuffer. One viewer who skipped through dozens of films made a healthy device model look broken.
- Release process: `docs/release-checklist.md`, `scripts/emulator_smoke.sh`.

Not in this release: the panel's `is_adult` flag (all 171 flagged channels are in the category the name rule already locks, so it would change nothing today), a baseline profile (needs a test account to cover more than the login screen), favourites stored with the account (needs a server feature).
