Kululu IPTV 1.5.87 brings the redesigned TV interface to the Android app and improves remote navigation, episode browsing and personalized discovery.

- Modern home, catalog, detail, player and settings screens, with improved episode cards, season navigation, artwork and cast photos when metadata is available.
- Live TV and radio use TS/MPEG-TS. HLS playback and fallback are removed; movies and episodes retain their native file formats.
- Improved rapid scrolling focus, remote-friendly EPG details, VOD seeking and resume position handling.
- Recent movies and series are limited to 50 items, with a new-content badge for the last 24 hours. Series freshness uses the latest episode's added timestamp when supplied by the panel.
- Recommendations are limited to 50 items and learn locally from each profile's actual viewing time. Personalization develops as viewing history accumulates.
- Login/content-loading stability improvements and compatibility fixes for older Android devices.

Android 5.0 or newer. Universal APK with ARM 32/64-bit and x86 32/64-bit support. Package: `com.iptv.player`; version code: `131`.

Validation: the feature candidate passed 612 Android unit tests and lint with no errors. Earlier device checks covered rapid navigation, EPG details, seeking, settings and cast photos. The final recommendation and episode-date changes have not yet been verified on the physical stick because it is offline. A reported audio-ahead-of-video issue remains under investigation; this release does not claim to resolve it.

The signed APK is available for manual installation. Automatic customer rollout is paused at 0% pending final device validation; the stable rollout version remains 1.5.86.
