Kululu IPTV 1.5.98 makes interlaced (1080i) channels watchable on weak Amlogic sticks and fixes three smaller playback behaviours found in an on-device log investigation of a Xiaomi MiTV Stick.

- Interlaced channels on the software route now play at full frame rate on Amlogic and other 32-bit sticks: libVLC's deinterlacer is off there (measured 10 fps → 25 fps). Hardware routes and progressive channels are unaffected. Operators can override this per device class with the new `vlcDeinterlace` policy key.
- On sticks whose hardware decoder cannot play interlaced H.264 (the picture froze after a few frames until the 7 s watchdog fired), the app remembers that fact and switches such channels to the software route as soon as the stream is identified, instead of waiting for the watchdog on every first visit.
- A 5xx answer from the panel while opening a channel (cold start, overload) is retried on the same player with the usual backoff instead of falling back to the software decoder, which could not help and kept the channel on that route.
- The media session no longer republishes identical playback states; the launcher and Bluetooth services stop receiving hundreds of updates per channel change.
- Diagnostics builds gain an instrumented video renderer and adb-settable test switches (documented in `docs/device-test-bench.md`); production builds do not include them.

Android 6.0 or newer (API 23). Universal APK with ARM 32/64-bit and x86 32/64-bit support. Production package: `com.iptv.player`; version code: `142`.

Validation: Android unit tests, lint, CI FFmpeg extension verification and the Room schema drift guard. The deinterlace change and the interlaced fail-fast were measured on a Xiaomi MiTV Stick (Amlogic s4, Android 11) with side-by-side preview builds.
