Kululu IPTV 1.5.97 hardens playback recovery, the update flow and playback diagnostics after the 1.5.96 engine upgrade.

- Media3's built-in stuck-player detection is now configured explicitly: live TV relies on the app's own stall watchdogs, and movies or episodes whose declared duration is shorter than the content play through to the real end instead of stopping after one minute. A detected stall retries on the same route.
- DVB subtitle tracks on live channels no longer trigger a playback error when selected.
- Update prompts are shown at most once a day per version after "Later", and a release that requires a newer Android version than the device has is never offered or downloaded; the About screen explains the requirement instead of reporting a generic failure.
- Pausing live TV from the remote, an audio-focus loss or a Cast hand-off is no longer reported to support as a video freeze; decoder type is reported correctly; idle devices report far less often.
- Movie and series refresh stop early when the panel is unreachable, throttled or rejects the account, never run twice in parallel, treat emptied categories correctly, and retry only what failed. Dashboard refresh can be abandoned and names the dataset that failed.
- Support centre capacity and freshness handling improved; measurements keep their original device timestamps.

Android 6.0 or newer (API 23). Universal APK with ARM 32/64-bit and x86 32/64-bit support. Production package: `com.iptv.player`; version code: `141`.

Validation: Android unit tests, lint, CI FFmpeg extension verification and the Room schema drift guard passed. Physical-device playback with these settings has not yet been revalidated.
