Behebt auf Fire TV Sticks und ähnlichen Geräten Sender, die sich kurz nach dem Start immer wieder schlossen und neu luden („Wird geladen…“) oder ruckelnd weiterliefen, obwohl andere IPTV-Apps sie auf demselben Gerät problemlos wiedergaben.

Details for maintainers (not published in the update prompt):

- Root cause: live TV on ExoPlayer confirmed the picture by sampling the video surface (PixelCopy). On sticks that composite decoded video on a hardware plane (Fire TV, Amlogic-class) the copy reads black, or a zeroed placeholder that classifies as solid green, so a healthy stream was declared invalid after about 9 s and the channel was reopened or moved to libVLC and on to its software decoder, while the same stick played the channel in other apps. The server saw clean, fully delivered streams: the loop happened entirely on the device (customer reports of 9 Oct 2026; an Android 7.1.2 stick measured on 26 Sep 2026).
- Fix: the pixel-only verdicts (startup solid green, persistent blank, 9 s validation deadline) are advisory while PixelCopy has not proven a healthy frame on the stream; Media3's own frame cadence (at least 24 frames, at least 8 in the last second, player READY, at least 1 s after the first frame, 3 s after a green sample) is accepted as video proof instead (`LiveSurfaceVerdictPolicy`, `NativeFrameCadence`). On Fire TV (`Build.MANUFACTURER` Amazon) this applies on every route by default; elsewhere only where no other video stage exists (constrained Amlogic profile), exactly as in the unreleased 1.5.99 draft. Decoder-side checks (frame stall, no first frame, audio underrun) are unchanged, so real stalls still recover.
- Remote per-device override `nativeFrameTrust` (docs/playback-policy.md): `false` restores the 1.5.98 strict verdicts for a device class, `true` makes them advisory everywhere. The crash-receiver (`telemetry-store.js`) must know the key before a rule uses it.
- Where the verdicts are advisory, a surface sample that blocks the main thread for 1.5 s on a weak device (4 s elsewhere) stops sampling for that stream instead of freezing the remote control during startup; shorter blocking samples are spaced out.
- During a retry backoff the failed ExoPlayer is stopped and its provider connection closed, instead of streaming and playing audio until the next attempt. The learned interlaced-stall quirk (1.5.98) leaves ExoPlayer early only when another route exists.
- Telemetry: reconnect, fallback, fatal and playback-attempt rows name the check that declared the video invalid and how the picture was confirmed (`proof=NATIVE_ADVISORY`); the app log line reads `PixelCopy advisory (...; Fire TV default) -> accept Media3 native frames`.
- Validation: Android unit tests, lint and the crash-receiver tests. Not verified on a physical Fire TV Stick: install the APK on one, open a channel that looped before and check the playback log (Settings → support diagnostic) for the accept line above. The rollout opens to every device on publication (owner decision of 10 Oct 2026, no staged percentage); the release-health gate still pauses it automatically if the fleet's QoE measurably degrades.

Android 6.0 or newer (API 23). Production package: `com.iptv.player`; version code: `146`.
